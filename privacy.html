Privacy Policy

Riddle Content Automation Tools

Effective27 Jul 2026

This tool is a private, single-operator automation system used to produce and publish short-form riddle videos to the developer's own YouTube and TikTok channels. It is not a public-facing app, and it does not collect information from any third party or end user.

01

What this is

"Riddle Content Automation Tools" is a set of scripts built and operated by a single creator to research, produce, and publish riddle videos, and to review their own performance analytics. It runs on the developer's own computer and connects only to accounts the developer owns.

02

Information we access

Through official platform APIs, this tool accesses:

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by YouTube (Google) and TikTok, scoped only to the developer's own connected channel.
  • Basic channel metadata — channel name, video list, and identifiers needed to publish content.
  • Performance analytics — views, watch time, engagement, and related metrics for videos on the developer's own channel.
03

How it's used

Data retrieved through these APIs is used exclusively to publish video content to the developer's own channels and to review that content's performance. It is never used for advertising, profiling, resale, or any purpose unrelated to operating these accounts.

04

Storage & security

Access and refresh tokens are stored locally, in a credentials file on the developer's own machine. They are never transmitted to or stored on any third-party server, never committed to source control, and can be revoked at any time from the respective platform's account settings.

05

Sharing

No data collected or accessed by this tool is sold, rented, licensed, or shared with any third party, for any purpose.

06

Third-party platforms

This tool interacts with YouTube and TikTok strictly through their official developer APIs and under their respective developer terms. Data handled on those platforms' own systems remains governed by Google's Privacy Policy and TikTok's Privacy Policy.

07

Retention & deletion

Tokens are retained locally only for as long as the tool is in active use. They can be deleted immediately by removing them from local storage, and access can be revoked at any time from within the YouTube or TikTok account settings.

08

Children's privacy

This tool is internal production tooling, not a consumer product. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

09

Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised as the tooling changes. The date above reflects the most recent revision.
